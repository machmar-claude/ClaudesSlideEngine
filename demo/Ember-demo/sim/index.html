Per LED: 4.77 mA peak (R_EXT 3.9 k), 1/16 duty → 0.283 mA at full. Firmware caps the average fill at 25 % (316 mA). Scan: 16 lines × 62.5 µs = 1 kHz refresh; one 8-bit line loads in 25.6 µs over 4 chains at 20 MHz. Values are 8-bit duty after a 2.2 gamma table, so the dim end of Breathe steps in whole duty units, as the real drivers do.
